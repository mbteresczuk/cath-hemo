How to Use

Step 1 · Enter Data

Scan: Tap "Take Photo" to photograph a cath sheet — values are extracted automatically. Atrial pressures (RA, LA) are converted to V/A format.

Type: Enter one location per line:

Location  Saturation  Systolic/Diastolic  Mean

SVC  79
RA   75  8/10  9
RV   75  50/5
MPA  75  50/30  38
RPCWP  12
LV   98  95/10
Aorta  98  95/55  72

Step 2 · Select a Diagram

Tap Select Diagram → then search by diagnosis (e.g. TOF, ASD, HLHS Norwood). Tap a card to select it.

Step 3 · Edit Diagram (optional)

Tap Edit Diagram to add devices if needed: stent, ASD/VSD device, PDA closure, BTT shunt, RV–PA conduit, thickened valve, SAPIEN or Melody valve, vascular plug, PA band, ECMO or VAD cannula, Fontan baffle, or Fontan fenestration. Drag to position, use ↻ to rotate, ⤡ to resize, ↔ to lengthen/shorten, ↕ to set the Fontan conduit's height, ✕ to delete. Skip this step entirely by tapping Complete Diagram → right after selecting a diagram.

Draw mode lets you sketch freehand on the diagram — each stroke becomes a movable object. Move #s shows draggable markers for where saturations (red) and pressures (blue) print; drag them to fix positions, which are remembered for that diagram. Sat/Press lets you type a value the program didn't recognize and drop it on the diagram as a circled saturation, a pressure with a line above it, or plain free text.

Step 4 · Complete Diagram

Tap Complete Diagram → to annotate the diagram (devices included), produce a hemodynamic narrative, and calculate Fick-derived values. Use ← Edit Diagram on the report screen to adjust devices and re-complete, or simply drag any printed saturation or pressure directly on the final image — it re-prints as a flat picture, and the change applies to that report only.

Step 5 · Export

  • Save Image — download the annotated diagram as PNG
  • Share — share via the system share sheet
