How to Use

Step 1 · Enter Data

Scan: Tap "Take Photo" to photograph a cath sheet — values are extracted automatically. Atrial pressures (RA, LA) are converted to V/A format.

Type: Enter one location per line:

Location  Saturation  Systolic/Diastolic  Mean

SVC  79
RA   75  8/10  9
RV   75  50/5
MPA  75  50/30  38
RPCWP  12
LV   98  95/10
Aorta  98  95/55  72

Step 2 · Select a Diagram

Tap Select Diagram → then search by diagnosis (e.g. TOF, ASD, HLHS Norwood). Tap a card to select it.

Step 3 · Edit Diagram (optional)

Tap ✏️ Edit Diagram to add devices if needed: stent, ASD device, VSD device, PDA closure, BTT shunt, or RV–PA conduit. Drag to position, use ↻ to rotate, ⤡ to resize, ↔ to lengthen/shorten a stent or conduit, ✕ to delete. Skip this step entirely by tapping Complete Diagram → right after selecting a diagram.

Step 4 · Complete Diagram

Tap Complete Diagram → to annotate the diagram (devices included), produce a hemodynamic narrative, and calculate Fick-derived values. Use ← Edit Diagram on the report screen to adjust devices and re-complete.

Step 5 · Export

  • Save Image — download the annotated diagram as PNG
  • Share — share via the system share sheet
  • Copy Narrative — copy the prose report to clipboard
Browse diagrams without entering data using View Diagrams.
